Shipping and Delivery

Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days and is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and order weight. Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available at an additional cost.

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days before shipping. You will receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.

International shipping is available to most countries, with delivery times varying by destination, typically 10-20 business days. Customs fees and import duties, if applicable, are the responsibility of the customer.

We are not responsible for delays caused by the shipping carrier, customs processing, or extreme weather events. If your package is marked as delivered but you have not received it, please contact support within 7 days.

Free shipping is available on orders over $75 within the continental service area.
