Privacy Policy

We collect information needed to create and maintain customer accounts, process orders, provide customer support, process payments, and deliver products.

Account information may include your name, email address, billing information, shipping address, and order history.

We use order and account information to provide services, respond to support requests, process refunds, and communicate important information about purchases or account activity.

Payment information is processed through our payment providers. We do not store complete payment card numbers on our systems.

We may retain account and order information for as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records.

You may request deletion of your account through Settings > Account > Delete Account. Some information may need to be retained when required by law or necessary for legitimate business purposes.

For privacy questions or requests, contact our support team.
