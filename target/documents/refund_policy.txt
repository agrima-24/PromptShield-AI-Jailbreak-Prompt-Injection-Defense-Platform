Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date. To be eligible, the item must be unused and in the same condition it was received, in its original packaging.

To start a refund, contact our support team with your order number and the reason for the return. Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

If approved, your refund will be processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is a result of our error.

Sale items are final and cannot be refunded unless defective.

For digital products, refunds are only available within 48 hours of purchase and only if the product has not been downloaded or activated.
