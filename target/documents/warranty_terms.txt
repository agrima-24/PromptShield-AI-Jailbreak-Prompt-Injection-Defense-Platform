Warranty Terms

Eligible physical products are covered by a limited warranty for 12 months from the original purchase date.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and failures that occur during normal intended use. It does not cover accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized modifications, normal wear and tear, or damage caused during return shipping.

To make a warranty claim, contact our support team with your order number, product information, and a description of the problem. Photos or other documentation may be requested to help diagnose the issue.

If a claim is approved, we may repair the product, replace it with the same or an equivalent product, or provide another resolution at our discretion.

Warranty replacements are shipped using standard delivery at no additional charge. Expedited shipping may be available for an additional fee.

A warranty claim does not automatically qualify for a refund. Refund requests are handled according to the Refund Policy.
