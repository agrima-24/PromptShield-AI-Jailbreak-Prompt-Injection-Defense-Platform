Security Practices

We use technical and organizational safeguards to protect customer account, order, billing, and support information.

Customer accounts are protected using password authentication and security controls designed to prevent unauthorized access. Customers should use a unique password and should not share account credentials.

Payment information is handled through third-party payment providers. Complete payment card numbers are not stored on our systems.

Access to customer information is limited to authorized personnel who need the information to provide services, process orders, resolve support requests, or maintain our systems.

We monitor systems for suspicious activity and investigate potential security incidents.

If you believe your account has been compromised, contact our support team as soon as possible and reset your password through the account login page.

We may require additional verification before making changes to account information or providing sensitive account details through support.
