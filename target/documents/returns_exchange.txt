Returns and Exchanges Policy

Customers may request a return or exchange within 30 days of the original purchase date. Items must be unused, in resalable condition, and returned with the original packaging and order information.

To request a return or exchange, contact our support team with your order number and the reason for the request. Support will provide return instructions and, when applicable, an exchange authorization.

Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the requested replacement is unavailable, customers may choose a refund instead.

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item was defective, damaged during delivery, or the return is required because of an error on our part.

Items purchased during a final-sale promotion are not eligible for return or exchange unless they are defective.

Digital products cannot be exchanged. Digital product returns are governed by the Refund Policy.
